Features

Immersive World

CYBERIA takes place in an alternative timeline set in the late 90s, with clothing, items, lore, and weapons to match. Experience the future today, exactly how it was dreamed in the past.

Flight System

Custom flight system utilising DayZ's physics engine to produce realistic simulation, with 30+ helicopters, planes, biplanes, and the legendary MV-22.

CYBERIA Weapons

Lasers, plasma bolt guns, disk launchers, flamethrowers, electrical weapons, the list goes on. 49+ retrofuturistic weapons made in-house; all CYBERIA exclusive.

Character Lab

A full custom character system allows players to configure hair, eye, brow, and beard colours, as well as select from various hair and beard styles.

No HUD + Custom UI

Our servers ship no HUD. An in-game Status Monitor becomes your stat readout: check it by holding H. Our sleek, minimal UI streamlines your survival and stays out of the way when it counts.

Dynamic Events

Tier-scaled events, dynamic crash sites, infected hoardes, and keycard missions, with minimal, balanced AI. Get lost in immersive custom scripted events and earn the best loot CYBERIA has to offer.

Groups

Get your squad on comms and band together in our Groups system. Press J at any time to open the groups menu and invite up to 4 friends. Verify on our Discord or donate to the server for exclusive perks!

Chernarus Island

The once-polar Chernarus has been reimagined as a tropical island, dense and lush with overgrown foliage. The cold is no longer a problem... but the heat is more of a challenge than ever.

Play

Search CYBERIA in the DayZ launcher or server browser. Only one workshop mod to install: CYBERIA.
